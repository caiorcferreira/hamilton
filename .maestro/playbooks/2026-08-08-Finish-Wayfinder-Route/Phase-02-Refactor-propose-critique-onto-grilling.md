  **Read complete — 0 images analyzed.** Read both `SKILL.md` and its disclosed `GLOSSARY.md`. Key takeaways that will govern the propose/critique edits:

  - **Duplication** (_failure mode_, glossary L177–181): the same meaning in more than one single source of truth. Costs maintenance, tokens, and inflates a meaning's prominence on the ladder past its real rank. Applied here: once `hamilton-grilling` owns the dialogue protocol (one question at a time, lead with a recommendation, look facts up rather than asking, never answer for the human), **every protocol sentence left in propose is duplication** — a second source of truth that will drift the moment grilling's protocol evolves. The end state is one authoritative place for the protocol; propose keeps only the *call* (delegate to grilling) plus the *question content* and *exit condition* the ticket assigns to the caller.
  - **Sediment** (_failure mode_, glossary L189–193): stale layers that settle because adding feels safe and removing feels risky; the slow erosion of relevance. This is the rot the route's craft-focus paragraph warns about. After the dialogue is extracted, propose must be read **end-to-end** — any half-instruction that referenced the now-removed protocol (e.g. "ask one question at a time, prefer multiple-choice" surviving in the Principles section, or the process-flow diagram still labelling step 4 "(one at a time)") is sediment and must go, not be trimmed around.
  - **No-op** (_failure mode_, glossary L196–201): an instruction that changes nothing because the model already does it by default. Post-delegation, any residual "be concise / one at a time / don't answer for the human" line in propose becomes a no-op *and* a duplication — grilling already enforces it. The no-op test is run **sentence by sentence**, not line by line; when a sentence fails, delete the whole sentence, don't word-trim it.
  - **Single source of truth** (glossary L171–175): the desired end state — each meaning in exactly one authoritative place. Duplication is its violation. This is the bar the review (later task) checks against: read edited steps end-to-end and confirm no protocol meaning lives in two places.
  - **Pruning discipline** (SKILL.md L53–59): keep each meaning in one place → check every line for relevance → hunt no-ops sentence by sentence. The order matters: relevance first (does this line still bear on what propose does *after* delegation?), then the no-op test on each surviving sentence. Be aggressive — most prose that fails should go, not be rewritten.
  - **Negation** caution (glossary L162–165): if propose's edited steps need to say what *not* to do, phrase the positive target instead. Relevant because the attendance guard ("unattended → fall back, don't invoke grilling") is cleaner as a positive branch ("unattended → record a reasonable choice as an assumption") than a prohibition.
  - **Leading words** (SKILL.md L61–72): the refactor is a chance to collapse any restated triads into a single pretrained token. If propose repeats the delegation idea in three sites, that's duplication begging to collapse — but the collapse target here is a *call* to grilling, not a re-description of its protocol.
