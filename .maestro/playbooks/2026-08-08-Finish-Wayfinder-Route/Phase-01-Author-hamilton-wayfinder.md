  - **Invocation trade-off:** model-invoked pays context load (description always in window) but is reachable by other skills; user-invoked pays cognitive load (human must remember) but zero context load. This maps to the co-location principle: keep the mechanics' definition, rules, and caveats under one heading.
  - **Leading words:** compact pretrained concepts that anchor behaviour in fewest tokens (e.g. _map_, _route_, _ticket_, _unit_). Hunt for restatements to collapse into a single token.
  - **Pruning:** single source of truth (one authoritative place per meaning); relevance (does the line bear on the task?); no-op test sentence-by-sentence (does it change behaviour vs default? — if not, delete the whole sentence). Be aggressive.
  - **Negation:** prompt the positive; keep prohibition only as a hard guardrail paired with what to do instead.
